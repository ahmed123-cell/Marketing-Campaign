The question

One send. Two rival explanations.

Kevin Hillstrom's email dataset randomly split 64,000 past customers into a men's-product email, a women's-product email, or nothing at all, then watched who visited the site and who bought. A higher conversion rate in the treatment groups is suggestive — but it's also exactly what you'd expect to see occasionally from chance, or from a broken randomization that quietly stacked better customers into one arm. This project runs the checks that tell the two apart: balance tests, hypothesis tests, and a regression that controls for everything else before crediting the email.

Before trusting the results, trust the split.
